This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and manage personal information when you access our educational platform, multimedia resources, community, ebooks and outsourcing services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use our platform, we may collect:

  • Account information such as your name, email address and username.
  • Profile information you choose to provide.
  • Course enrolment, learning progress and lesson interactions.
  • Community posts, comments, feedback and uploaded content.
  • Ebook requests, purchases, downloads or access records, where applicable.
  • Outsourcing enquiry details, project briefs, files and communications.
  • Technical information such as browser, device, log and usage data.
  • Information you voluntarily submit through contact or support forms.

2. How we use your information

We may use information to:

  • Operate and maintain our website and services.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide courses, lessons, videos and learning resources.
  • Respond to enquiries, support requests and feedback.
  • Facilitate community interactions and collaboration.
  • Manage ebook access and content-related requests.
  • Review, plan and fulfil outsourcing service requests.
  • Improve website performance, accessibility and user experience.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access and security incidents.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Learning activity and educational data

We may process information about your learning activity, such as courses viewed, lessons accessed, progress, quiz responses or saved resources, to provide learning functionality and improve the educational experience.

Where available, learning progress may be associated with your account to help you continue where you left off.

4. Ebooks and educational content

When you browse, request, download, purchase or access ebooks and other educational content, we may process related information to manage access, fulfil requests, maintain records and respond to content enquiries.

We may also process information submitted by ebook authors, contributors, publishers or content owners concerning content submission, permissions, review and publication.

5. Outsourcing and project services

If you request outsourcing, professional, creative, technical, research, educational or content-related services, we may collect information such as your contact details, project requirements, specifications, files, deadlines, communications and relevant transaction details.

This information is used to assess your request, communicate with you, coordinate work, provide agreed services and maintain necessary business records.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your project.

6. How information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information when reasonably necessary with:

  • Hosting, storage and infrastructure providers.
  • Website, analytics and technical service providers.
  • Payment providers, where payments are offered.
  • Project collaborators or service providers, where required to fulfil a request.
  • Authorities or other parties where legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety.

Where appropriate, we take steps to ensure service providers handle information under suitable confidentiality and security obligations.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, support website functionality, understand platform usage and improve performance.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some features.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of processing of, or other actions concerning your personal information. You may also have rights concerning consent and certain automated processing.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

10. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining records, resolving disputes, protecting the platform and meeting legal obligations.

11. Children's privacy

Our educational content may be useful to learners of different ages. Where personal data relates to a child, we will handle it in accordance with applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards. Certain account, community or interactive features may have age restrictions.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our platform, services, practices or legal obligations. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding your privacy or personal information, contact our team.
